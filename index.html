From the sketchbook

Every tile has its own painting

22 property cards painted for Moropoly, from the riverside homes of Sidi Moussa to the Twin Center in Maarif, plus the 3okacha gate and ten silver tokens. They all appear in the game: on the board, in the deed cards and in the reveal when you land.
